# Neuroscience and Brain Function

The human brain contains approximately 86 billion neurons connected by trillions of synapses. This complex network enables consciousness, memory, learning, and emotion through electrochemical signaling.

Neuroplasticity allows the brain to reorganize throughout life by forming new neural connections. Different regions specialize: the hippocampus for memory, amygdala for emotion, and prefrontal cortex for decision-making.

Advanced imaging techniques like fMRI and EEG map brain activity. Research into Alzheimer's, Parkinson's, and mental health disorders continues advancing. Brain-computer interfaces like Neuralink aim to restore movement and communication for paralyzed patients.
