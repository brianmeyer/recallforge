# US National Parks Guide

The National Park Service manages 63 national parks protecting America's natural heritage. Yellowstone, established in 1872, was the world's first national park. The system spans diverse ecosystems from glaciers to deserts.

Iconic parks include Grand Canyon's layered geology, Yosemite's granite cliffs and waterfalls, and Glacier's remaining ice fields. Wildlife viewing opportunities include bears, wolves, bison, and elk in their natural habitats.

Planning requires advance reservations for popular parks. Backcountry permits allow wilderness camping. Leave No Trace principles minimize human impact. The best times avoid summer crowds and extreme weather. The America the Beautiful pass provides annual access to all federal recreation sites.
