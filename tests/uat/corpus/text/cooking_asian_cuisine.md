# Asian Cooking Techniques

Asian cuisines emphasize balance of flavors: sweet, sour, salty, bitter, and umami. Wok cooking over high heat creates wok hei, the smoky breath of the wok essential to Cantonese dishes.

Rice is foundational across East and Southeast Asia. Japanese sushi showcases vinegared rice with fresh fish. Thai curries balance coconut milk, curry paste, and aromatics. Korean fermentation produces kimchi and doenjang.

Knife skills are paramount. Vegetables are cut uniformly for even cooking. Proteins are sliced against the grain for tenderness. Sauces combine soy, fish sauce, oyster sauce, and rice vinegar in precise ratios. Steaming, stir-frying, and braising preserve nutrients and textures.
