# Cardiology and Heart Health

The cardiovascular system includes the heart, blood vessels, and blood. The heart pumps 5 liters of blood per minute through 60,000 miles of vessels, delivering oxygen and nutrients while removing waste.

Common conditions include coronary artery disease, heart failure, arrhythmias, and valve disorders. Risk factors include hypertension, high cholesterol, smoking, diabetes, and family history. Prevention emphasizes diet, exercise, and smoking cessation.

Diagnostic tools include ECG, echocardiography, cardiac catheterization, and MRI. Treatments range from medications like statins and beta-blockers to interventions including stents, pacemakers, and heart transplants. Cardiac rehabilitation helps patients recover after events.
