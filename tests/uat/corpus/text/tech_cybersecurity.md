# Cybersecurity Best Practices

Modern cybersecurity requires defense in depth: multiple layers of security controls across networks, endpoints, and applications. Zero trust architecture assumes breach and verifies every access request regardless of origin.

Key practices include multi-factor authentication (MFA), encryption at rest and in transit, regular patching, and least privilege access. Phishing remains the top attack vector, making user training essential.

Advanced threats use AI for automated attacks and social engineering. Security teams deploy SIEM tools, EDR solutions, and threat intelligence feeds. Incident response plans must be tested regularly through tabletop exercises and red team engagements.
