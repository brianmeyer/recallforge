# Mountain Environments and Alpine Ecology

Mountain ranges form through tectonic plate collisions, volcanic activity, and erosion. The Himalayas, Andes, Alps, and Rockies shape global weather patterns by forcing air masses upward, creating rain shadows and microclimates.

Alpine ecosystems above the tree line experience extreme conditions: intense UV radiation, high winds, thin soil, and short growing seasons. Specialized plants like edelweiss, alpine cushion plants, and lichens cling to rocky surfaces. Mountain goats, snow leopards, and pikas inhabit these harsh environments.

Glaciers store freshwater that feeds rivers supporting billions of people downstream. Climate change is causing rapid glacial retreat, threatening water supplies. Mountain hiking, climbing, and skiing bring tourism revenue but require careful management to protect fragile alpine environments.
