# Renewable Energy Technologies

Renewable energy comes from naturally replenishing sources: solar, wind, hydroelectric, geothermal, and biomass. These sources produce minimal greenhouse gas emissions compared to fossil fuels.

Solar photovoltaic panels convert sunlight directly to electricity. Wind turbines harness kinetic energy from air movement. Both technologies have seen dramatic cost reductions, making them competitive with coal and natural gas.

Energy storage through lithium-ion batteries and pumped hydro addresses intermittency challenges. Smart grids balance supply and demand in real-time. The transition to renewables requires infrastructure investment and policy support to achieve net-zero emissions targets.
