# Yoga Philosophy and Practice

Yoga originated in ancient India as a spiritual discipline. Modern practice emphasizes physical postures (asanas), breathing techniques (pranayama), and meditation for holistic wellness.

Different styles suit different needs. Vinyasa links breath with flowing movement. Hatha holds poses longer for alignment focus. Ashtanga follows set sequences with vigorous practice. Restorative yoga uses props for deep relaxation.

Physical benefits include improved flexibility, strength, and balance. Mental benefits reduce stress through parasympathetic activation. The eight limbs of yoga include ethical guidelines, physical practice, and ultimately spiritual liberation. Regular practice builds body awareness and present-moment focus.
