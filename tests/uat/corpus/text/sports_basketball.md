# Basketball Strategy and Training

Basketball is played 5-on-5 on a 94-foot court. Modern NBA offenses emphasize spacing, three-point shooting, and pick-and-roll actions. Analytics have transformed the game, favoring efficient shots at the rim and behind the arc over mid-range jumpers.

Defensive schemes include man-to-man, zone (2-3, 3-2, 1-3-1), and switching defense. The shot clock creates urgency, requiring quick decision-making. Fast break transition offense converts turnovers and rebounds into easy baskets.

Training combines strength work, agility drills, shooting repetitions, and film study. Player development focuses on skill versatility: big men who can shoot threes, guards who can defend multiple positions. The draft, free agency, and salary cap shape team building strategies.
