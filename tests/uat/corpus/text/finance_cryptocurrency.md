# Cryptocurrency and Digital Assets

Cryptocurrencies are digital currencies using cryptography for security. Bitcoin, created in 2009, introduced decentralized peer-to-peer transactions without intermediaries. Ethereum added smart contracts enabling decentralized applications.

Blockchain technology maintains distributed ledgers across networks. Mining and staking secure proof-of-work and proof-of-stake systems. Wallets store private keys controlling asset access. Exchanges facilitate trading but carry custody risks.

Volatility characterizes crypto markets. Regulatory frameworks evolve globally. Institutional adoption grows through ETFs and corporate treasuries. DeFi protocols enable lending, borrowing, and trading without traditional banks. NFTs represent unique digital ownership. Risks include hacks, scams, and regulatory uncertainty.
