# Climate Change and Global Warming

Climate change refers to long-term shifts in global temperatures and weather patterns. Human activities, primarily burning fossil fuels, have increased atmospheric carbon dioxide by 50% since the Industrial Revolution.

Global average temperatures have risen 1.1°C above pre-industrial levels. Effects include melting ice caps, rising sea levels, extreme weather events, and ecosystem disruption. The Paris Agreement aims to limit warming to 1.5°C.

Mitigation strategies include renewable energy transition, carbon capture, reforestation, and sustainable agriculture. Climate models use supercomputers to project future scenarios. Adaptation measures protect vulnerable communities from unavoidable impacts.
