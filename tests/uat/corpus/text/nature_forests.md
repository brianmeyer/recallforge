# Temperate Forest Ecosystems

Temperate forests cover vast regions of North America, Europe, and East Asia. Deciduous species like oak, maple, beech, and birch shed leaves in autumn, creating spectacular fall colors. Coniferous forests feature evergreen pines, spruces, and firs.

The forest floor hosts decomposers like fungi and bacteria that recycle nutrients. Understory plants include ferns, mosses, and wildflowers that bloom before the canopy closes in spring. Old-growth forests contain trees over 200 years old and provide critical habitat.

Wildlife includes deer, bears, foxes, owls, and woodpeckers. Forest ecosystems regulate climate by sequestering carbon dioxide. Threats include logging, fragmentation, invasive species, and wildfire exacerbated by climate change. Reforestation and sustainable forestry practices help restore degraded areas.
