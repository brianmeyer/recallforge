# Road Cycling and Racing

Road cycling combines endurance, power, and tactical acumen. Grand Tours like the Tour de France span three weeks with mountain stages, time trials, and sprint finishes. Professional cyclists average 25-30 mph over varied terrain.

Training zones target different physiological adaptations. Zone 2 builds aerobic base. Sweet spot training improves FTP (functional threshold power). High-intensity intervals develop VO2 max.

Equipment matters: carbon fiber frames balance stiffness and weight. Deep-section wheels reduce aerodynamic drag. Electronic shifting provides precise gear changes. Nutrition strategies prevent bonking on long rides. Drafting in pelotons reduces energy expenditure by 30% compared to solo riding.
