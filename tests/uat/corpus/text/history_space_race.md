# The Space Race

The Space Race was a Cold War competition between the United States and Soviet Union for space supremacy (1957-1975). It began with Sputnik 1, the first artificial satellite, shocking American confidence.

Soviet achievements included first human in space (Yuri Gagarin) and first spacewalk. NASA responded with Project Mercury, then Gemini practicing rendezvous and docking. The Apollo program culminated in the moon landing on July 20, 1969.

Neil Armstrong and Buzz Aldrin walked on the lunar surface while Michael Collins orbited above. Five more missions landed, with Apollo 13's aborted mission demonstrating NASA's problem-solving. The race ended with Apollo-Soyuz cooperation, leading to the International Space Station.
