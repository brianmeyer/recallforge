# Mastering the Charcoal Grill

Charcoal grilling produces superior flavor through the Maillard reaction and smoke. Use lump hardwood charcoal for the cleanest burn. Arrange coals in a two-zone setup: hot side for searing, cooler side for indirect cooking.

For steaks, sear over direct high heat for 3-4 minutes per side, then move to indirect heat until desired doneness. Rest the meat for 5 minutes before cutting. Use an instant-read thermometer: 130°F for medium-rare, 140°F for medium.

Wood chips like hickory, mesquite, cherry, and apple add distinct smoke flavors. Soak chips for 30 minutes before adding to coals. Keep the lid closed to trap smoke. Low and slow barbecue at 225°F transforms tough cuts like brisket and pork shoulder.
