# Jazz Music and Improvisation

Jazz originated in New Orleans combining African musical traditions with European harmony. Key elements include swing rhythm, blue notes, call-and-response, and improvisation. The genre evolved through Dixieland, swing, bebop, cool jazz, and fusion.

Improvisation is central to jazz expression. Musicians create spontaneous melodies over chord progressions. Standards provide common frameworks for jam sessions. Great improvisers like Charlie Parker and John Coltrane developed signature vocabularies.

Ensembles range from solo piano to big bands. The rhythm section (piano, bass, drums) provides harmonic and temporal foundation. Horn players develop tone and technique for expressive phrasing. Jazz education now includes university programs preserving and extending the tradition.
