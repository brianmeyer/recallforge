# Sourdough Bread Baking Guide

Sourdough bread uses a natural starter culture of wild yeast and lactobacilli instead of commercial yeast. Maintaining a starter requires daily feedings of flour and water. A healthy starter should double in size within 4-6 hours after feeding.

The autolyse technique mixes flour and water before adding the starter, improving gluten development. Bulk fermentation takes 4-8 hours depending on temperature. During this time, perform stretch-and-fold sets every 30 minutes.

Shape the dough into a boule or batard, then cold-retard overnight in the refrigerator for flavor development. Bake in a preheated Dutch oven at 500°F for 20 minutes covered, then 20 minutes uncovered for a crispy crust.
