# Soccer Tactics and Formations

Soccer formations describe how 10 outfield players are arranged. The 4-3-3 provides width with wingers. The 4-2-3-1 balances defense with a creative number 10. The 3-5-2 uses wingbacks for attacking width.

Pressing high up the pitch to win the ball back quickly defines gegenpressing, popularized by Jurgen Klopp. Tiki-taka emphasizes short passing and ball retention, as seen in Pep Guardiola's Barcelona. Counter-attacking teams absorb pressure then exploit space on the break.

Set pieces from corners and free kicks account for roughly 30% of goals. Video analysis and GPS tracking data help coaches optimize player positioning and workload. Youth academies develop players from age 8 through professional contracts, focusing on technical skill and tactical awareness.
