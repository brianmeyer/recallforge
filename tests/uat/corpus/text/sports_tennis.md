# Tennis Strategy and Technique

Tennis is played on hard courts, clay, and grass, each surface affecting ball bounce and speed. The modern game emphasizes baseline power with topspin groundstrokes hit with western grips.

The serve initiates every point. Effective serves combine pace, spin, and placement. The second serve typically uses kick or slice for safety. Return positioning anticipates serve direction.

Strategy varies by opponent. Aggressive baseliners dictate points with depth and power. Counter-punchers absorb pace and redirect angles. Serve-and-volleyers attack the net. Mental toughness separates champions, managing momentum shifts and pressure points in tight matches.
