# Reading Architectural Blueprints

Architectural blueprints are technical drawings that communicate design intent to builders. Floor plans show room layouts, dimensions, and wall positions from above. Elevations depict exterior facades. Sections cut through the building to reveal internal structure.

Scale is critical: residential plans typically use 1/4 inch = 1 foot. Symbols indicate doors, windows, electrical outlets, plumbing fixtures, and HVAC equipment. Title blocks contain project information, revision history, and professional stamps.

Detail drawings zoom into specific assemblies like wall sections, stair connections, and waterproofing details. Construction documents include specifications that describe materials, finishes, and installation methods. Together, plans and specs form the contract documents for construction.
