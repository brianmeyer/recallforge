# Modern Surgical Techniques

Surgery has evolved from open procedures to minimally invasive techniques. Laparoscopy uses small incisions and cameras, reducing recovery time and complications. Robotic surgery provides enhanced precision and 3D visualization.

Common procedures include appendectomy, cholecystectomy, joint replacement, and cardiac bypass. Preoperative assessment evaluates anesthesia risks. Sterile technique prevents surgical site infections. Postoperative care manages pain, mobility, and wound healing.

Specializations include neurosurgery, orthopedic, cardiothoracic, and plastic surgery. Trauma surgery addresses acute injuries. Transplant surgery replaces failing organs. Advances in imaging, biomaterials, and regenerative medicine continue expanding surgical capabilities.
