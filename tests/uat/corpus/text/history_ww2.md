# World War II Overview

World War II (1939-1945) was the deadliest conflict in human history involving over 30 countries. Axis powers Germany, Italy, and Japan fought Allied powers led by Britain, the Soviet Union, and the United States.

The war began with Germany's invasion of Poland. Blitzkrieg tactics conquered Western Europe. Japan attacked Pearl Harbor bringing America into the Pacific theater. Turning points included Stalingrad, Midway, and D-Day.

The Holocaust systematically murdered six million Jews and millions of others. Atomic bombs dropped on Hiroshima and Nagasaki ended the Pacific war. The United Nations formed to prevent future conflicts. The war reshaped global power, ending European dominance and beginning the Cold War.
