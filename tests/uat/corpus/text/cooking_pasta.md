# Homemade Pasta from Scratch

Making fresh pasta at home requires just flour, eggs, salt, and olive oil. Use 00 flour or all-purpose flour for the dough. Form a well in the flour, crack eggs into the center, and gradually incorporate the flour with a fork.

Knead the dough for 8-10 minutes until smooth and elastic. Rest it covered for 30 minutes. Roll thin using a pasta machine or rolling pin, then cut into desired shapes: fettuccine, pappardelle, or filled ravioli.

Cook fresh pasta in heavily salted boiling water for just 2-3 minutes. It cooks much faster than dried pasta. Toss immediately with your sauce. Classic pairings include carbonara, bolognese, cacio e pepe, and aglio e olio.
