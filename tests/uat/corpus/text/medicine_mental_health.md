# Mental Health and Psychiatry

Mental health encompasses emotional, psychological, and social well-being. Common disorders include depression, anxiety, bipolar disorder, schizophrenia, and PTSD. One in five adults experience mental illness annually.

Treatment combines psychotherapy and pharmacotherapy. Cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT) changes thought patterns affecting behavior. SSRIs, SNRIs, and other medications balance neurotransmitters. Treatment-resistant cases may benefit from ECT or TMS.

The biopsychosocial model recognizes biological, psychological, and social factors. Stigma reduction encourages help-seeking. Crisis intervention prevents suicide through hotlines and emergency services. Integrated care addresses mental and physical health together.
