# Modern Architecture and Design

Modern architecture emphasizes form follows function, clean lines, and honest use of materials. Pioneers include Le Corbusier, Mies van der Rohe, Frank Lloyd Wright, and Zaha Hadid. Glass curtain walls, reinforced concrete, and steel frames define the aesthetic.

The International Style spread modernism globally after World War II. Brutalism used raw concrete for monumental government buildings and housing blocks. Postmodernism reacted against modernist austerity with historical references and playful decoration.

Contemporary sustainable architecture integrates green roofs, passive solar design, natural ventilation, and recycled materials. Parametric design uses computational algorithms to generate complex organic forms. BIM (Building Information Modeling) software enables integrated design and construction workflows.
