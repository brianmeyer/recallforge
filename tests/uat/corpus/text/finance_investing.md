# Investment Strategies and Portfolio Management

Investing puts capital to work for returns through stocks, bonds, real estate, and alternatives. Modern Portfolio Theory optimizes risk-adjusted returns through diversification. Asset allocation matches investments to time horizons and risk tolerance.

Equity strategies include value investing (buying undervalued companies), growth investing (high-growth potential), and index investing (market returns with low fees). Dollar-cost averaging reduces timing risk through regular purchases.

Risk management uses position sizing, stop losses, and hedging. Fundamental analysis evaluates company financials. Technical analysis studies price patterns. Behavioral finance recognizes cognitive biases affecting decisions. Long-term compounding generates wealth while minimizing transaction costs and taxes.
