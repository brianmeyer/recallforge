# Traveling in Japan

Japan blends ancient traditions with cutting-edge modernity. Tokyo offers neon-lit districts, world-class dining, and efficient transit. Kyoto preserves temples, gardens, and geisha districts. Osaka is known for street food and comedy culture.

Transportation centers on the JR Pass for tourists and punctual bullet trains (shinkansen) connecting cities. Convenience stores (konbini) provide surprisingly good meals. Onsen (hot springs) offer relaxation with specific etiquette.

Seasonal considerations include cherry blossoms (late March-early April), autumn foliage, and humid summers. Cash remains king despite technological advancement. Language barriers exist outside tourist areas but locals are helpful. Respectful behavior includes removing shoes, quiet train rides, and proper chopstick use.
