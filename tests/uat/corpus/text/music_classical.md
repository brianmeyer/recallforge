# Classical Music History

Classical music spans from medieval to contemporary periods. The Baroque era (1600-1750) featured Bach, Handel, and Vivaldi with ornate polyphony and harpsichord. The Classical period (1750-1820) brought Mozart, Haydn, and early Beethoven with balanced forms.

Romantic music (1820-1900) emphasized emotion and individual expression through Chopin, Brahms, and Wagner. The 20th century saw Stravinsky's rhythmic innovations and Schoenberg's atonality. Orchestras grew from chamber ensembles to symphony-sized forces.

Musical forms include sonata-allegro, theme and variations, fugue, and symphony. Notation evolved to capture increasingly complex compositions. Performance practice research informs historically informed interpretations using period instruments.
