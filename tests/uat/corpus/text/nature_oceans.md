# Ocean Marine Biology

Earth's oceans cover 71% of the planet's surface and contain 97% of its water. Marine ecosystems range from sunlit coral reefs to pitch-dark deep-sea trenches. Phytoplankton produce half the world's oxygen through photosynthesis.

Coral reefs are among the most biodiverse ecosystems, supporting thousands of fish species, sea turtles, and invertebrates. Ocean currents like the Gulf Stream transport heat globally, influencing weather and climate patterns.

Deep-sea creatures have evolved remarkable adaptations: bioluminescence, extreme pressure tolerance, and chemosynthesis near hydrothermal vents. Overfishing, plastic pollution, and ocean acidification from CO2 absorption threaten marine ecosystems. Marine protected areas and sustainable fishing quotas are key conservation strategies.
