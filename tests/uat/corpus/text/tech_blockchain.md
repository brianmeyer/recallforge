# Blockchain Technology and Smart Contracts

Blockchain is a distributed ledger technology maintaining immutable records across peer-to-peer networks. Each block contains cryptographic hashes linking it to previous blocks, creating tamper-evident chains.

Smart contracts are self-executing programs on blockchains like Ethereum. They automatically enforce agreements when conditions are met, eliminating intermediaries. Use cases include decentralized finance (DeFi), supply chain tracking, and digital identity.

Consensus mechanisms like Proof of Work and Proof of Stake validate transactions. While Bitcoin pioneered the technology, modern blockchains focus on scalability, energy efficiency, and interoperability between chains.
