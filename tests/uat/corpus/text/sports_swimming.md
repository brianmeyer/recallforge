# Competitive Swimming Techniques

Swimming efficiently requires minimizing drag and maximizing propulsion. The four competitive strokes are freestyle, backstroke, breaststroke, and butterfly. Each has specific technique requirements and turn rules.

Freestyle uses alternating arm recovery with flutter kick. The catch phase anchors the hand to pull the body past. Body rotation reduces drag and engages core muscles. Breathing happens every two, three, or four strokes.

Starts and turns are crucial in short races. The dolphin kick underwater provides explosive propulsion. Training includes aerobic base work, lactate threshold sets, and sprint intervals. Technique drills isolate specific movements for muscle memory development.
