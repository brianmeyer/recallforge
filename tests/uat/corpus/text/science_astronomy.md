# Astronomy and Deep Space Exploration

Astronomy studies celestial objects and phenomena beyond Earth's atmosphere. The observable universe spans 93 billion light-years containing hundreds of billions of galaxies, each with billions of stars.

Telescopes like Hubble and James Webb capture images of distant galaxies, exoplanets, and cosmic events. Webb's infrared capabilities reveal the early universe, only 300 million years after the Big Bang.

Space exploration missions study Mars, asteroids, and outer planets. The search for extraterrestrial life focuses on habitable zones where liquid water could exist. Dark matter and dark energy remain mysterious, comprising 95% of the universe's mass-energy content.
