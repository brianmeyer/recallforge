# Clinical Nutrition and Dietetics

Nutrition science studies how food affects health and disease. Macronutrients (carbohydrates, proteins, fats) provide energy and building blocks. Micronutrients (vitamins, minerals) enable metabolic processes in smaller quantities.

Balanced diets include fruits, vegetables, whole grains, lean proteins, and healthy fats. Mediterranean and DASH diets reduce cardiovascular risk. Plant-based diets lower environmental impact while meeting nutritional needs through careful planning.

Clinical dietitians treat conditions through medical nutrition therapy. Diabetes management balances carbohydrate intake with medication. Renal diets restrict sodium, potassium, and phosphorus. Enteral and parenteral nutrition support patients unable to eat normally.
