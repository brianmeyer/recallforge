# French Pastry and Desserts

French pastry requires precision in measurements, temperatures, and techniques. Laminated doughs like croissants fold butter into layers, creating flaky textures through steam expansion during baking.

Custards range from light crème anglaise to thick pastry cream. Tempering eggs prevents curdling when adding hot liquids. Chocolate work requires tempering for glossy finishes and proper snap.

Classic desserts include macarons (meringue-based almond cookies), éclairs (choux pastry with cream filling), tarts with seasonal fruits, and soufflés that rise dramatically from whipped egg whites. Plating considers color, height, and sauce placement for visual appeal.
