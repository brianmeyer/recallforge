# Ancient Roman Civilization

Rome transformed from a small city-state to a Mediterranean empire spanning three continents. The Republic (509-27 BCE) featured elected consuls and the Senate. The Empire (27 BCE-476 CE) concentrated power in emperors.

Roman engineering built roads, aqueducts, and concrete structures that endure today. The Colosseum hosted gladiatorial combat and public spectacles. Roman law influenced modern legal systems through concepts like presumption of innocence.

Military organization enabled expansion. Legions of citizen soldiers conquered Gaul, Britain, and the Near East. Internal factors including political instability, economic troubles, and barbarian invasions contributed to the Western Empire's fall.
