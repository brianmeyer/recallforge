# The Renaissance Period

The Renaissance (14th-17th centuries) marked Europe's transition from medieval to modern. Originating in Italian city-states like Florence, it revived classical learning and emphasized human potential.

Artistic innovations included linear perspective, chiaroscuro lighting, and anatomical accuracy. Masters like Leonardo da Vinci, Michelangelo, and Raphael created enduring works. Patronage from wealthy families like the Medici funded artistic production.

Scientific inquiry challenged accepted wisdom. Copernicus proposed heliocentrism. Galileo improved the telescope and confirmed celestial movements. The printing press spread knowledge rapidly. The Renaissance laid groundwork for the Scientific Revolution and Enlightenment.
