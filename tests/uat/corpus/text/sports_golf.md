# Golf Fundamentals and Course Management

Golf requires technical skill, strategic thinking, and mental composure. The full swing generates power through proper sequencing: hips initiate, followed by torso, shoulders, arms, and finally club release.

Short game separates good from great players. Chipping uses various clubs with different trajectories. Bunker shots require open faces and steep swings to splash sand. Putting reads green contours and speed for line selection.

Course management minimizes big numbers. Laying up to preferred yardages avoids hazards. Understanding wind effects adjusts club selection. The mental game involves pre-shot routines, staying present, and accepting outcomes. Scratch golfers average par; professionals go significantly lower.
